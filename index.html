About the Gathering

More Than a Service. A Movement of Love.

Swollen Sunday is First Love Church's annual mega-gathering where thousands converge to experience vibrant worship, uncompromised preaching, and genuine community. On November 15, 2026, young people and families filled the UG Stadium in Legon for an unforgettable encounter with God.

Whether you gave your life to Christ, visited for the first time, or came to worship, you are part of the family.
